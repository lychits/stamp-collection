# Research Notes: Catalogue and market values for the album

**Status:** complete with documented limitations  
**Depth:** Deep

## Plan

- **Question:** Which issues are visible among the 103 cropped stamps, and what defensible current market ranges can be attached to each record?
- **Scope:** Identify from the three album photos, verify catalogue fields on specialist sites, and estimate collector-market ranges with used/postally cancelled condition noted. No authenticity grading or formal appraisal.
- **Audience:** The collection owner.
- **Deliverable:** Enriched stamp cards in the collection app, with sources and uncertainty in each record.

## Findings Log

Album photos show bird-themed issues from Cuba, USSR, Vietnam, Nicaragua, and European issuers. Several stamps bear cancellations and are photographed in plastic sleeves. Specialist thematic catalogues establish several exact issue dates, denominations, and species; others remain provisional.

## Conflicts & Open Questions

Some crops contain overlapping sleeve seams, cancellation marks, or neighboring stamps. A catalog value for a mint stamp cannot be treated as a sale price for these used examples.

## Limitations

Neither the presence of gum nor the condition of individual perforations can be evaluated through the photographed sleeves. The 7 small Polish crops were grouped into one composition pending a sharper scan. No matching completed-sale prices for these exact copies were verified: the displayed USD ranges are **indicative mixed-lot estimates**, anchored by dealer *offers*, not independently demonstrated realization prices. The 25 saved research pages include repeated catalogue pages and do not provide 25 independent price sources. See `research/catalogue-and-market-values.md`.
